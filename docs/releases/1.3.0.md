# Party Mix DJ 1.3.0

Free, dedicated DJ software for the **Numark Party Mix** controller.

## New
- **Loop Control + Loop Slicer** panel: loops from 1/32 to 32 beats (in / out / auto / move / double / halve / re-trigger, beat-grid quantised) and a slicer with 4, 8 or 16 pads (one-shot, loop, hold, quantised; per-slice reverse and repeat), running inside the audio engine, sample-accurate and click-free.
- **XY Multi-FX pad**: Filter / Echo / Reverb / Flanger blended by the pointer position, neutral centre, instant clean release; each of the four has a selector with 7 effects (28 in all, 21 new: comb, vowel, peak, ping-pong, tape, dub, slapback, reverse and triplet echo, hall, plate, spring, gated, swell, dark reverb, phaser, chorus, vibrato, tremolo, auto-pan, ring mod); assignable zones, configurable ranges and presets; mouse, trackpad and touch.
- **36 pad effects** (20 new: Tape Stop, Spinback, Reverse, Trance Gate, Machine Gun, Beat Roll, Tape Echo, Vibrato, Sidechain Pump, Lo-Fi, Megaphone, Underwater, Noise Riser, Robot, Jet, HP Sweep, Bass Kill, Lows Only, Octave Down / Up). Fixed: the Stutter effect was silent.
- **Harmonic Match advisor**: reads the playing song, plots the best next songs on a Camelot wheel and ranks them with the reasons.
- The waveforms are mouse controls: click = play / pause, drag = rewind / forward, wheel = zoom, shift + wheel = nudge.
- New one-screen layout: wide waveforms between two half-size visuals at the margins, loop panel and XY pad beside the mixer, playlist at 40 % of the window, AUTO DJ centred in the top bar.
- Sharper, beat-locked visuals (device resolution, speaker models with realistic cabinets), party lights that pulse on the beat, richer waveform (vivid colours, bar numbers, intro / outro zones, ruler).
- Right volume fader: a filter for the worn top end of the fader track (no more jumping at the top); PAD MODE per deck follows the controller; headphone volume on the CUE MIX knob; treble / bass boost +20 %; vinyl-style brake on pause; automatic cue points after analysis; M3U / radio playlists; PC folder browser.

## Install
Download `PartyMixDJ-Setup-1.3.0.exe` (installer) or `PartyMixDJ-1.3.0-portable.exe` (no install). Windows may warn about an unknown publisher (the app is not code-signed): **More info → Run anyway**.

## Notes
Made for the original Numark Party Mix. The PAD MODE LEDs, the headphone jack output and the new loop / slicer / XY panels have had limited testing on the real controller: please report anything that behaves differently on your unit.
